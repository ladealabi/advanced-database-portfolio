SCENARIO: COMMIT: an atomic transfer becomes visible only after COMMIT
==============================================================================
Session A transfers N10,000 from Ada (8880000001) to Bayo (8880000003) inside
an explicit transaction. Session B observes before and after A commits.
Atomicity: the debit, the credit, the txn row and both ledger legs are applied
together. Durability: after COMMIT the change is in the WAL and survives a
crash.
