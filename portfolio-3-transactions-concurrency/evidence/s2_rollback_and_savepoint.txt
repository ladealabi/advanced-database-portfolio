SCENARIO: ROLLBACK: explicit rollback, automatic rollback on error, and SAVEPOINT
==============================================================================
(1) An explicit ROLLBACK undoes a completed transfer. (2) A transfer that
breaks a business rule (insufficient funds) raises an error, the whole
transaction is aborted, and nothing can run until ROLLBACK. (3) A batch of
three payments uses SAVEPOINTs, so the one failing payment is rolled back on
its own and the other two still commit.
