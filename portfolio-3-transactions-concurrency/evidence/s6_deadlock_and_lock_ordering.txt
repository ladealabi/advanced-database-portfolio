SCENARIO: CONCURRENCY CONTROL: deadlock detection, and prevention by lock ordering
==============================================================================
Part 1: naive transfer code debits the source first. A sends Ada->Bayo while B
sends Bayo->Ada at the same time. A locks Ada and waits for Bayo; B locks Bayo
and waits for Ada. This is a cycle in the wait-for graph. PostgreSQL's
deadlock detector (after deadlock_timeout = 1s) aborts one victim. Part 2: the
same two transfers through transfer_funds(), which always locks the lower
account_id first. B simply waits, then both succeed, with no deadlock.
