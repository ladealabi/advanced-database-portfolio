SCENARIO: STRESS TEST: 16 concurrent clients calling transfer_funds() for 20s
==============================================================================

constantly, so without lock ordering this would deadlock. The invariants checked afterwards:
money is conserved, every txn is balanced, no negative balances, and no new deadlocks.
