SCENARIO: CONCURRENCY CONTROL: SERIALIZABLE (SSI) prevents the write skew
==============================================================================
KYC tier-1 rule (customer level, not account level): Ada may send at most
N60,000 per day across ALL her accounts. Two requests arrive at the same
moment: N40,000 from her SAVINGS to Bayo (session A) and N40,000 from her
CURRENT account to a merchant (session B). Each checks the daily total, sees
N0 used, and proceeds. The two transactions read the same data but write
DIFFERENT rows, so row locks and REPEATABLE READ (snapshot isolation) cannot
see a conflict. Under SERIALIZABLE, PostgreSQL's Serializable Snapshot
Isolation tracks the read->write dependencies between the two transactions (a
'dangerous structure') and aborts one with SQLSTATE 40001. The application
retries it, the re-check now sees N40,000 already used, and the request is
declined.
